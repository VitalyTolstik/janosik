## Residual risks

- SMTP acceptance and the database commit are separate operations. An acceptance reply lost in transit, or a crash after acceptance and before the database commit, can cause a repeat. Retries reuse the same Message-ID. The timestamps reflect acceptance by the configured SMTP server and downstream bounces are outside this system.
- Permanently rejected deliveries remain pending and retry with a delay capped at five minutes. Completed rows shed their payload and are pruned by maintenance. Rows marked before the migration retain their historical timestamps because actual past delivery cannot be inferred.
- Announcement recipients are captured at publication. Account notification recipients are rechecked against the current address, preferences and permissions before sending. A grouped reminder is canceled if the recipient has lost access to any of its boards.
- Schedule recovery restores future ticks. Digests pick up unreserved attention events still retained in PostgreSQL. Activity summaries and due reminders continue to use their configured time windows.
- A definitive command rejection is still shown as a failure. When both replies and the snapshot are unavailable, the tab relies on SSE replay or the next successful online/focus refresh.
- SMTP and throttle edits require a coordinated restart on the other nodes. Frontend rebuilds served by nginx require regenerating its CSP include.
