# Trademark policy

The **code** in this repository is licensed under the
[GNU AGPL-3.0](./LICENSE). That licence covers the source — it does **not**
grant any right to the project's **name** or **logo**. This policy explains what
you may and may not do with the Janosik marks. It is a short, plain-language
summary, not legal advice.

## The marks

"**Janosik**", the Janosik wordmark and the "Перо Яношика" feather logo (the
**Marks**) identify this project and the hosted service run by its maintainer.

## You may, without asking

- **Use the name to refer to the project** — in articles, tutorials, talks,
  comparisons, package metadata ("works with Janosik") and the like.
- **Redistribute the software unmodified** under the Janosik name, as long as it
  is clearly the unmodified upstream build and links back to this repository.
- **Fork and modify** the code — under a **different name and logo**. Say your
  project is "based on Janosik" or "a fork of Janosik"; just don't call the fork
  itself "Janosik".

## You may not, without written permission

- Use the Marks (or a confusingly similar name or logo) for your own product,
  service, company or domain.
- Offer a modified or hosted version **under the Janosik name**, or in any way
  that suggests it is the official Janosik or endorsed by the maintainer.
- Use the Marks in a way that implies sponsorship, affiliation or endorsement
  you do not have.

In short: **fork the code freely — just don't fork the brand.** Only the
maintainer's hosted offering may be presented as the official Janosik service.

## Third-party assets

Some bundled assets (fonts, libraries) carry their own licences — see the
respective `package.json` files and upstream projects.
